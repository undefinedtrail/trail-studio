Custom Hand-Thrown Ceramics Studio

Bespoke earthenware shaped on the potter's wheel and fired in small batches. Choose classic Indian silhouettes and tactile mineral glazes.

🔥 Lead Time & Firing Protocol

Hand-formed on the potter's wheel. Bisque fired at 980°C and reduction glazed. Strict 14-day minimum crafting window.

Standard Atelier Firing Cycle

Every piece undergoes an attentive 5-stage studio cycle before handover.

  • 1
    Throwing (Day 1–2) Clay wedging and hand-forming on the electric potter's wheel.
  • 2
    Leather Trimming (Day 3–5) Foot-ring carving, handle pulling, and slow ambient sun drying.
  • 3
    Bisque Firing 980°C (Day 6–8) Dehydration in kiln transforming raw clay into porous stoneware.
  • 4
    Mineral Glazing (Day 9–11) Hand-dipping and brushing with natural mineral oxide suspensions.
  • 5
    High-Fire & Annealing (Day 12–14) Vitrification firing and gradual kiln cooling to room temperature.
